## How to Add an Entry

Copy the template below, fill in what you know, and append it to this file. Leave fields blank
rather than guessing.

```markdown
### <summary>
- **Type / Author / Date:**
- **What:**
- **Why:** what improves, or what breaks without it.
- **Evidence:** files, commits, PRs, or the incident.
```
